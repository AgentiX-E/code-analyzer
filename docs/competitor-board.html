What the pair can and cannot say. Two arms scored by one judge against one set of goldens is a comparison of two reviewers. If the two rows do not carry the same model, it is also a comparison of two models and the reader is entitled to know that before reading the ordering.

Resolution. A paired difference on the AACR slice is resolvable at 0.0126 F1 (4.8 goldens, §147.3); a difference smaller than that is not claimed, however true it is.
